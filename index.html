    General strategy

    1. Each attempt is a genome: genes in 0…1 that decode into the athlete's movement.
    2. A fitness score rewards getting closer to the goal, with a big bonus for success.
    3. Every new attempt is bred immediately: tournament selection, crossover and Gaussian mutation.
    4. A child that beats the weakest member replaces it, so the best attempt is never lost.
    5. Mutation size adapts. If learning stalls, random immigrants are injected.

    Sport-specific strategy

      About half of the new attempts use this "coach's feedback". Switch the strategy off to compare against a pure GA.
